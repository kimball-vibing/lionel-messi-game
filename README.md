# Where in the World Is Lionel Messi?

A sports spin-off of "Where in the World Is Cameron SanFrancisco?", made for Deskin Behrend.

You're a scout for the G.O.A.T. Scouts (Getting Our All-stars Together). A sports GOAT has slipped away right before the big game.
Follow sports clues from city to city, work out who it is with the Scouting Computer, get a Draft, and bring them to the stadium
by next Wednesday at 5 PM. Draft every GOAT and rank up to Ace Scout to take on the final case: Lionel Messi himself.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a web browser. Use the arrow keys and Enter (Esc goes back), or click and tap.

## Files

- `index.html`: the game screens and rules
- `clues.js`: the sports clues for each city
- `sound.js`: sound effects and music, made with Web Audio
- `art/landmarks.js`, `art/people.js`, `art/sprites.js`: the pixel art
